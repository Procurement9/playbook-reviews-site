Frequently asked questions

Do I really need active noise cancellation?

If you commute, fly, or work in noisy spaces, yes — it changes the experience entirely. For quiet home use, skip ANC and save money.

How long do wireless headphone batteries last?

Modern pairs deliver 30-40 hours; older designs hit 20. Look for pairs with fast charging (5 minutes for 2 hours) if you travel often.

Are expensive pairs really worth it?

Above $400 you pay mostly for materials and branding. The $180-$350 range offers nearly all the real quality benefits.
