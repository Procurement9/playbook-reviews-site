About & Methodology

Who we are, how we test, and why you can trust the recommendations.

Our 5-Point Review Standard

1. We personally test and live with products for months before publishing a ranking.
2. We always publish real cons — never a sanitized "everything is great" review.
3. We never accept payment, free product with strings, or pre-approval from any brand.
4. We disclose affiliate relationships on every page, up front.
5. We revisit and re-score guides as products change.

How This Site Makes Money

When you buy through links on our site, we may earn a small affiliate commission at no extra cost to you. This is our only revenue source, and it is the reason we can keep the site free and ad-free. It never changes which product wins a guide.

Corrections & Contact

Found something we got wrong? We update guides within 48 hours of credible feedback. We also accept reader questions about upcoming purchases — honest opinions only.
